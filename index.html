The Privacy Doctrine

Last Updated: May 2026

At Habit Gallery, we believe that consistency is the ultimate aesthetic, and true discipline requires absolute silence. This Privacy Doctrine outlines our unwavering commitment to the privacy, security, and cryptographic isolation of your digital atelier.

1. The Archival Principle: Absolute Local Storage

Habit Gallery operates entirely as an offline, private sanctuary. We do not collect, harvest, transmit, or store your personal data, habit progress, or usage analytics on any external servers. Your daily check-ins, masterpiece completions, and gallery interactions are written exclusively to your device's local storage. You are the sole curator of your history.

2. Patronage and the Digital Vault (In-App Purchases)

When you choose to support the gallery through Patronage (In-App Purchases) to acquire tokens or aesthetic artifacts, the financial transaction is securely processed and authenticated by the Google Play Billing system. Habit Gallery never has access to your credit card or financial details. To ensure industrial-grade security against unauthorized tampering, your acquired digital assets (Tokens) are sealed within your device's highest-tier security enclave.

3. Equipment Bound Policy & Non-Migratability of Assets

Due to the strict cryptographic isolation of the device's hardware enclave, all unconsumed Digital Assets (Tokens) are legally and technically tethered exclusively to the specific physical device on which they were purchased.

  • The Boundary of Vault Synchronization: The "Vault Synchronization" (local archive export) feature is strictly designed to migrate historical habit logs, check-in timelines, and naturally unlocked exhibition manifests.
  • The Permanent Forfeiture Clause: Digital Assets (Tokens) are strictly excluded from the archive file and cannot be transferred, backed up, or migrated. Uninstalling the application, clearing application sandbox data, or migrating to a new physical device will result in the permanent forfeiture of all unconsumed Digital Assets remaining on that specific device. The Gallery bears no liability for recovering or converting such assets under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Services

While Habit Gallery acts as a sealed environment, we rely on Google Play Billing SDK for patronage processing, which may collect basic diagnostic or purchase validation data as governed by Google's overarching privacy policies.
