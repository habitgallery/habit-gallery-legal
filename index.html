Part I — Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Day Canvas mobile application and the legal information page linked from the application and its Google Play listing.

Day Canvas does not require an account and does not operate a developer-controlled backend for storing your habit or gallery data.

2. Information handled by the app

Day Canvas may handle the following information on your device so that its features can work:

  • habit names and tracking preferences that you enter;
  • Journey schedules, check-ins, rest days, timer progress, and completion history;
  • artwork progress, fragments, cracks, repairs, frames, signatures, and collection records;
  • locally earned token balances, inventory, and reward claim states;
  • notification times and notification enablement preferences; and
  • local settings needed to remember whether introductory or review-related prompts have already been attempted.

This information is used only to provide the app’s habit tracking, artwork collection, reminder, reward, backup, and restoration features.

3. Local storage

Day Canvas stores its primary app data locally in the application storage assigned to it by the operating system. Token balances use platform-provided secure storage where available.

Day Canvas does not operate a server that receives your habit names, check-in history, artwork signatures, gallery activity, or token balance. We do not sell this information and do not use it for advertising.

The device operating system or app store may provide device-level backup or restore features according to your device settings and the platform provider’s policies. Those platform services are controlled by the relevant platform provider, not by Day Canvas.

4. Vault export and import

Vault Synchronization is a user-initiated local export and import feature.

An exported Vault file may contain:

  • Journey and habit records;
  • check-in and artwork progress;
  • collection and frame information;
  • local inventory information; and
  • Theme reward claim states.

Token balances are not included in Vault exports.

When you export a Vault file, Day Canvas creates a JSON file and opens the operating system’s share interface. You choose where the file is saved or which external application receives it. Day Canvas does not automatically upload the file to us. After you share or store a Vault file outside Day Canvas, the selected destination’s privacy and security practices apply.

You are responsible for protecting exported Vault files because they may contain habit names and activity history that you consider private.

5. Tokens and digital rewards

Tokens in Day Canvas are earned through local app activity. In the current version:

  • tokens cannot be purchased with real money;
  • tokens cannot be sold, transferred, withdrawn, or exchanged for cash, goods, or external benefits;
  • tokens have no monetary value; and
  • token balances are excluded from Vault exports.

Clearing app storage, uninstalling the app, changing devices, or losing access to the device may result in the loss of locally stored tokens.

6. Notifications

If you enable The Daily Call, Day Canvas requests notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. Reminder times and enablement preferences are stored locally.

Day Canvas may access the device’s configured time zone so that reminders can be scheduled at the intended local time. The app does not request precise or approximate location permission for this purpose.

You can disable reminders in Day Canvas or change notification permission in Android system settings.

7. Saving artwork and accessing files

Day Canvas allows you to save generated Collection Cards or original artwork views to your device. The app does not require broad access to read your photo library for this feature.

When you import a Vault file, the operating system file picker lets you select the file. Day Canvas reads the selected file only to perform the restoration you requested.

8. Network connections and third-party platform services

Day Canvas does not include advertising, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Google Play Billing in the current version.

The app may interact with the following platform or third-party services:

  • Google Play distribution and In-App Review: Google Play may process device, account, diagnostic, or interaction information under Google’s own terms and privacy policies. Day Canvas does not receive the content of a review through the in-app review prompt.
  • Google Fonts: Some app versions or device states may request font resources provided by Google. Google may receive technical connection information, such as an IP address, when responding to such a request. Day Canvas does not receive this information.
  • External browser: When you choose Terms & Privacy, Day Canvas asks your browser to open the public legal page. Your browser and network provider handle that connection.
  • Operating system share and file interfaces: When you export, import, or save content, the operating system and any destination you select process the action according to their own policies.

Day Canvas does not control the independent data practices of Google, your operating system provider, browser, cloud storage provider, or another application you choose through a system interface.

9. Analytics, advertising, and purchases

The current version of Day Canvas:

  • does not use analytics SDKs to track your behavior;
  • does not use crash-reporting SDKs that transmit crash data to us;
  • does not display third-party advertisements;
  • does not access an advertising identifier; and
  • does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions.

If a future version changes these practices, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated before that version is released.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until it is changed through normal app use, cleared through Android system settings, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to any device-level backup or restore service controlled by the platform provider.

Vault files that you export are controlled by you and must be deleted from their saved locations separately.

Because Day Canvas does not provide user accounts or store your app data on a developer-controlled server, there is no server-side account or cloud profile for us to delete.

11. Security

Day Canvas relies on application sandboxing, platform secure storage, and operating system permissions to protect local data. No method of storage is completely secure. You should protect access to your device and store exported Vault files only in locations you trust.

12. Children’s privacy

Day Canvas is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a developer-controlled server. If you believe a child has provided information directly to us, contact us so that we can review the request.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s features, SDKs, legal obligations, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised “Last updated” date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, contact habitgalleryapp@gmail.com.
